Phishing attacks trick users with fake invoices, urgent account warnings, malware attachments, or prize scams, all designed to steal money, passwords, or personal data. Common signs include spoofed domains, urgency, generic greetings, grammar errors, and too-good-to-be-true offers. High-risk cases like vendor fraud, credential theft, and malware can cause major financial or system damage. The key defense is vigilance—verify requests, avoid clicking suspicious links, report phishing, update software, and use MFA. Ultimately, phishing is a human problem as much as a technical one, requiring awareness and safe email habits to stay protected.
